Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 September 2026

H.Collage collects nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of ours for the app to talk to. Your photos stay on your phone.

Who this is from

H.Collage is made by NG Hive (Natalia Gurevich), an individual developer. Questions about this policy go to dona.ios18@gmail.com.

What the app keeps on your device

  • The photos you pick for a collage, held only while you are editing.
  • The collage you export — saved to your Photos library, at your request.
  • The date you first opened the app and whether you have bought it, so the 14-day trial can be counted.
  • If you set up the private section: its contents, encrypted with a key derived from the code you choose. That code is never stored.

All of this lives on your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.

What leaves your device

Making a collage sends nothing anywhere. The app has no backend and does not upload your photos.

Inside the private section, two things do use the network, and both go where you point them: requests to the file server whose address you enter, and pages you open in the built-in browser. Those reach the operators of those servers and websites directly, under their own privacy policies. They do not pass through us and we never see them. Server passwords are typed each time and are not stored.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

There are none. The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no tracking identifiers, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Your purchase

The one-time purchase is processed by Apple, not by us. Apple handles your payment details; we never receive them. Apple provides developers with aggregated sales reports that do not identify individual buyers.

Permissions the app asks for

Access to your photo library, used only so you can choose photos for a collage and save a finished one back. The app does not read your library in the background and does not ask for location, contacts, microphone or camera.

Children

H.Collage is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Since the app collects no information from anyone, this is a matter of intended audience rather than data handling.

If you write to us

Email you send us contains whatever you choose to put in it, and we keep it only as long as needed to answer you. We do not add you to any mailing list.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of the State of Florida, United States.
