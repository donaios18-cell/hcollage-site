The small things

what it does, precisely

Framing
Drag a photo to move it inside its own frame and pinch to zoom in. It can't be dragged past its edge, so a gap never opens up.
Swapping
Press and hold any photo to replace it or put it back the way it started. A plain tap does nothing — that gesture belongs to dragging.
Borders
One switch puts thin white gutters between the photos, or takes them away so the edges meet.
Shape
Square 1:1, vertical 4:5 or horizontal 3:2 — switched at any point, with the layout previews taking the same shape so you see what you'll get.
Export
Save straight to Photos, or hand it to the iOS share sheet. JPEG, 2048 px on the longer side.
Offline
Making a collage sends nothing anywhere. There is no server, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads.
Requires
iPhone on iOS 16.4 or later.

And a private section

H.Collage keeps one more room. Entering a numeric code — one you choose at setup — in that same "how many photos" field opens a private section holding a browser and access to your own file server. Any other number just makes a collage, or the usual "enter a number from 2 to 10".

What that section stores is encrypted with a key derived from your code, which is never itself saved. Server passwords are typed each time and kept nowhere. The section locks itself the moment the app leaves the foreground.

Said plainly: this guards against someone picking up your phone and looking through it. It is not built to withstand a forensic examination of a device that has been seized and unlocked, and nothing at the app level honestly could be.
